## :sparkle: What it does

- :text: Schedule a message with `/later 2h hello`, and Discord sends it for you at that time.
- :star: Takes a delay (`2h`, `in 30 minutes`), a clock time (`18:30`) or a date (`2026-12-24 18:00`).
- :eye: Lists and cancels what is waiting with `/laters`.
- :arrow-up: Keeps sending while Discord is in the background, through Unbound's background refresh.

## :gear: Settings

- :color-palette: **Notify when sent** shows a toast as each scheduled message goes out. On by default.
- **Queued messages** caps how many can wait at once.
- **Default delay** is the fallback the schedule button uses when you give no time.
