## :sparkle: What it does

- :timer: Adds **Remind me** to the long-press menu of a message.
- :clock: Picks a delay from the preset sheet: in 10 minutes, in 1 hour, in 3 hours, or tomorrow morning.
- :bell: When it comes due, shows a toast with an **Open** button that jumps to the message.
- :mobile: On iOS it also posts a system notification if Discord is in the background.

## :gear: Settings

- **System notification** controls the background notification on iOS. On by default.
