## :sparkle: What it does

- :arrow-up: Adds **Download voice message** to the long-press menu of a voice message.
- :text: Saves the audio to your Downloads folder on Android, named `voice-message-<id>.ogg`.
- :star: On iOS it writes the file into Unbound's documents folder and offers it through the share sheet.

## :gear: Notes

- Only appears on messages Discord flags as voice messages, so a normal audio upload is left alone.
