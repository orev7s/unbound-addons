## :sparkle: What it does

- :timer: Sets you idle, do not disturb or invisible after Discord has been quiet for a while.
- :sliders-3: The delay, the status and every exception are yours to choose.
- :calendar: **Active hours** limit idle to a time window.
- :bell-slash: **Quiet hours** force a status of their own, ignoring the delay.
- :msg: Sending a message, coming back to Discord, being in a voice call or running an activity can each count as activity.

## :gear: Settings

- **Idle after** is how long Discord must be quiet first.
- **Idle status** is what shows once you are idle.
- **Only while Discord is in the background**, **Skip while in a voice call**, **Skip while an activity runs** and **Reset when you send a message** are the exceptions.
- **Active hours** and **Quiet hours** are the two schedule windows, each with its own start and end.

## :triangle-warning: Notes

- The status is set through Discord's own status setter. If a future Discord build moves it, the add-on logs one warning and leaves your status alone.
