Mutes chosen channels or servers on a schedule.

## What it does

- :bell-slash: During the window, mutes the channels and servers you list, and unmutes them when it ends.
- :clock: A window may cross midnight: a start later than the end runs into the next morning.
- :calendar: Each window counts the day it starts on, so a Friday-night window reaches into Saturday morning.
- :eye: Checks every 30 seconds while Discord is open.

## Settings

- **Mute on a schedule** turns the feature on or off. On by default.
- **Start** and **End** are 24-hour times, such as `22:00` and `08:00`.
- **Days** are the days a window starts on.
- **Channels and servers** are the ids to mute while quiet.
