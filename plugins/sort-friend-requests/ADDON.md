## :sparkle: What it does

- :user-plus: Orders the pending friend requests Discord shows, newest first by default or oldest first.
- :stopwatch: Uses the date Discord stores on a request, and falls back to the time in the user's id when it stores none.
- :sort-down: Leaves friends, blocks and every other relationship exactly where they were.

## :gear: Settings

- **Order** puts the newest requests at the top, or the oldest.
- **Sort suggested friends** also orders the suggested friends list, where Discord exposes one.
